# 🐸 The Princess and the Frog
### My Favorite Disney Movie

> "Almost there!" – Tiana

## 📌 About the Movie

**The Princess and the Frog** is an animated Disney movie released in 2009. It tells the story of Tiana, a hardworking young woman who dreams of opening her own restaurant in New Orleans.

Her life takes an unexpected turn when she meets Prince Naveen, who has been transformed into a frog because of a magical curse. After a surprising kiss, Tiana also turns into a frog, and they begin an adventure to find a way to become human again.

Throughout their journey, they discover the true meaning of love, friendship, courage, and believing in their dreams.

## 🎭 Main Characters

Here are some of the main characters in the movie:

- **Tiana** – A hardworking and independent young woman who dreams of owning a restaurant.
- **Prince Naveen** – A charming prince who learns the importance of responsibility and love.
- **Charlotte La Bouff** – Tiana's childhood best friend who dreams of becoming a princess.
- **Dr. Facilier** – The villain who uses dark magic to achieve his plans.
- **Ray** – A kind-hearted firefly who believes in true love.
- **Louis** – A funny alligator who dreams of becoming a jazz musician.

## ❤️ Why I Love This Movie

I love *The Princess and the Frog* because it has a beautiful story, memorable characters, amazing songs, and meaningful lessons.

What I admire most about Tiana is her determination to achieve her dreams. She reminds me that success does not happen overnight and that hard work is important in reaching our goals.

I also love how the movie combines romance, comedy, adventure, and music. It is entertaining while still teaching us valuable lessons about life.

**My favorite message from this movie is that dreams can come true when we work hard, believe in ourselves, and never give up.**
